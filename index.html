How to Use TagForge

Upload & Select Location

Start by uploading your photo. Then either search for a place or click anywhere on the map to set the exact location.

Understand Map Modes

Use 🗺️ Normal mode to find your location with city names and roads. After selecting the exact spot, switch to 🛰️ Satellite mode to view it in real-world imagery.

Note: Satellite mode does not display labels, so always select your location in Normal mode first for accurate positioning.

Customize & Download

Adjust overlay text, position, and style as needed. Once ready, click download to save your final geotagged image.

⚠️ Your privacy matters. This website does not collect, store, or upload any of your data. All processing happens locally in your browser.
